Finding 1 · RQ1

73.3% of delay minutes are airline-controlled

73.3%
airline-controlled delay
bootstrap 95% interval: 73.0–73.6%

Stable across the decade — the yearly internal share moves only between 69.7% (2018) and 77.4% (2022).

Scope: flights ≥15 min late only · source: BTS Delay Cause
发现一：73.3% 延误分钟归因于航空公司可控原因，十年稳定。
Finding 2 · The tail

A few flights carry most of the delay

0.876
Gini index of delay minutes
near-perfect concentration

So we report percentiles and the tail, and recompute every headline with the pandemic years excluded — the shift is reported, not hidden.

Lorenz curve · delay concentration · 61.7M completed flights
发现二：Gini 0.876，延误高度集中，少数航班承担大部分延误。
Finding 3 · RQ2

Delay is inherited within the operating day

38.8%
late aircraft
the single largest cause

6.0% of flights sit in a rotation already carrying delay; 17.1% of those minutes are inherited from an earlier leg. Delay accumulates toward evening.

Within-day cascade · delay propagates across the day
发现三：晚到飞机是最大单一原因（38.8%），延误在一天内级联累积。
Findings 4 & 5 · RQ3 / RQ4

Five profiles · risk ranked, not promised

RQ3
Airport archetypes
k = 5 over 195 airports; silhouette 0.2045. Reported unstable — ARI 0.69 vs a 0.70 threshold.
RQ4
Severe-delay risk
AUC 0.685 (logit) / 0.691 (GBM) at a 6.1% base rate. Departure hour = 49.9% of importance.
Clustering reports its evidence · model reports baseline, held-out, ranks-not-predicts
发现四：机场分五类，但如实报告为不稳定。发现五：风险可排序，不能承诺到达时间。

Quality & Honesty

How we judge it · what it cannot say

Quality gates
  • counts reconcile · 53 tests pass
  • baseline + held-out + ranks, not predicts
  • usability on 3 timed tasks
Stated limits
  • R² 0.022 → rank, not promise
  • cause minutes: ≥15 min flights only
  • observational → ranking, not causation
Success criteria as a checklist · limits stated, not hidden
